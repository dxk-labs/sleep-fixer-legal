Common questions

How does the sleep plan work?

Sleep Fixer shifts your bedtime gradually — 15 to 30 minutes a night — until you reach your goal schedule, instead of asking you to jump straight there.

What happens if I miss a night?

Nothing bad. Sleep Fixer never guilts, shames, or penalizes you for a missed night — it just builds a gentle recovery path back onto your schedule.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Every install works fully on its own. Signing in is optional and only useful if you want your plan to follow you to a second device.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app, go to Profile → Sleep Fixer Pro, and use the restore option there. If that doesn't work, email us at the address above with the email or account you purchased under.

How do I delete my data?

From Profile, you can reset your plan and progress at any time. If you'd like your account fully deleted, email us and we'll take care of it.
