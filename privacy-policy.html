Sleep Fixer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Sleep Fixer ("the app") is built with one rule above everything else: it never guilts, shames, or pressures you about your sleep — and that same respect extends to your data. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

No account required

Sleep Fixer does not require you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying information to use the app.

What's stored on your device

Your sleep schedule, goals, streaks, and progress history are stored locally on your device only. This data is not uploaded to, or stored on, any server we operate. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted along with it.

AI Coach

When you use the AI Coach feature, the messages you send and relevant context (like your sleep schedule) are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. This is the only way the AI Coach can work. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information (like medical record numbers, financial details, or passwords) in your messages. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Gemini API terms.

Notifications

Sleep Fixer uses OneSignal to deliver reminder notifications (like bedtime and wind-down reminders). OneSignal assigns your device an anonymous subscription identifier to make this possible — this identifier is not linked to your name or any personal account. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's system settings, and can decline the notification permission entirely without losing access to any core feature of the app.

Purchases

Optional purchases (such as unlocking extra visual themes or becoming a Founding Supporter) are processed through RevenueCat, which relies on Apple's App Store or Google Play billing to actually handle payment. We never see or store your card number or other payment details — that's handled entirely by Apple/Google. RevenueCat assigns your device an anonymous purchaser ID to keep track of what you've unlocked; it is not linked to your name or email.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • We don't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We don't require or ask for your real name, email, or phone number.

Children's privacy

Sleep Fixer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
